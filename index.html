Questions

Frequently asked.

What is protein coffee?

Kaapi Labs combines real coffee with high-quality dairy protein, giving you the coffee experience you already enjoy with added protein built into the cup.

Does it taste like a protein shake?

That's exactly what we're trying to avoid. Kaapi Labs is being developed coffee-first, with the goal of keeping the cup rich, smooth and familiar rather than turning it into a traditional protein shake.

Can I drink Kaapi Labs hot?

Yes. Kaapi Labs is being developed for your everyday hot coffee ritual and can also be enjoyed cold.

How much protein is in one serving?

Our current product target is approximately 10 g of protein per serving.

How many calories are in one serving?

Our current product target is approximately 60 calories per serving.

How much caffeine is in one serving?

Kaapi Labs is currently designed to provide approximately 60 mg of caffeine per serving, giving you a balanced coffee boost for your morning routine.

Is Kaapi Labs vegetarian?

Yes, Kaapi Labs is vegetarian.

Where will Kaapi Labs be available?

Kaapi Labs will launch across India, shipping to most pin codes. Early-access members get first pick of the launch batch.

When is Kaapi Labs launching?

We're preparing our first launch. Join the early-access list and we'll let you know first.
