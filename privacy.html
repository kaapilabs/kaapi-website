Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Kaapi Labs ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through kaapilabs.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. This policy is written to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

What we collect

Right now, the only personal information we collect is:

  • Your email address, when you join our early-access list.
  • Basic technical information that your browser sends automatically (such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited), collected through standard analytics.

We do not collect names, phone numbers, addresses, or payment details on this site. When we begin selling products, this policy will be updated and you will be told before any additional information is collected.

Why we collect it

We use your email address only to:

  • Send you updates about Kaapi Labs, including our launch date, tasting opportunities, and early-access offers.
  • Occasionally ask for your feedback on the product as we develop it.

We use technical information to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Who processes your data

Your email address is stored and processed by EmailOctopus, our email service provider. You can review their privacy practices at emailoctopus.com/privacy-policy.

Site analytics are handled by standard providers such as Google Analytics. These providers may store cookies on your device to recognise repeat visitors. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address on our early-access list until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it. Analytics data is retained according to the settings of the tools we use, typically no longer than 26 months.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act and comparable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct any information that is inaccurate.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Withdraw your consent for us to contact you at any time. Every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@kaapilabs.com.

Security

We rely on our service providers to keep your information secure, using industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest. No system is completely secure. If we ever become aware of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities promptly.

Children

Kaapi Labs is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated by email to people on our early-access list.

Contact

For any questions about this policy or your personal information, write to us at hello@kaapilabs.com.
